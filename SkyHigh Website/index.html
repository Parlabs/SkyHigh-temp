Getting Started

Up & flying in
three steps

No experience required. Whether you want to fly as a virtual pilot or guide traffic as an air traffic controller, we’ll get you there.

Step 01
Create Your Account
Head to the join page and register for a free account. It takes less than two minutes and gives you access to the entire global network.
Register for SkyHigh
Step 02
Download a Client
Install the free pilot client or SkyRadar ATC client. Both connect directly to the SkyHigh network and work with any major flight simulator.
Download clients
Step 03
Connect & Fly
Connect to the network and begin flying into UK & Ireland airspace, or start your ATC training journey and work toward controlling at major airports.

About the Network

What makes
us special?

We are a free-to-use, friendly network with AI traffic and AI controllers to fill the world when it gets quiet. We put our members first, providing real-time air traffic control for some of the busiest virtual airspace in the world.

Real Procedures
Fly real-world SIDs, STARs, and approaches based on current UK CAA charts, FAA, ICAO, and NATS publications.
24/7 Coverage
Controllers are on frequency around the clock thanks to our AI-powered controller system, especially during peak periods and major events.
Thriving Community
Growing membership, regular events, a welcoming Discord server, and dedicated mentors for new controllers in each division.
